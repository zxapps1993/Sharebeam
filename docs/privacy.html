Sharebeam is provided by zxapps ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use Sharebeam on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV, contact us, or visit this privacy-policy website. It applies to users worldwide. 1. Our approach to privacy

Sharebeam lets you share a screen and, when enabled, device audio directly with nearby devices you approve. You do not need a Sharebeam account. We do not operate a cloud relay or a server that receives your shared screen or audio. Sharebeam does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or an AI service that receives your content.

Local processing and sharing with your chosen recipients are still uses of information. Apple services, support email, and this website also have the separate data flows described below. This policy does not mean that no information is ever processed or transmitted.

2. Screen content and optional audio

When you start sharing, Sharebeam processes the screen, display, window, or other content permitted by Apple's capture interface. Depending on the platform, sharing may include the whole screen and may continue while you switch apps or until you stop the system broadcast. Visible information can include notifications, names, messages, photos, documents, and other personal or sensitive information.

If you choose to play sound on a receiving device, Sharebeam also processes supported application or system audio. Sharebeam has no microphone-sharing feature and does not use or transmit microphone audio. If Apple's broadcast controls supply microphone samples, Sharebeam's extension discards them. Apple TV acts as a receiver of content shared from another device.

Sharebeam encodes and transmits this content to the device or devices you approve, then decodes and displays or plays it there. Media is temporarily buffered to support live transmission and playback. Sharebeam does not provide a feature that saves a recording or uploads this media to zxapps. We do not receive, view, or store your live screen or audio through an app backend.

Only share content you are entitled to disclose. Review the selected screen, close private material, and consider hiding notifications. A recipient can see or hear the content you send and may independently photograph, record, or redistribute it using tools outside Sharebeam. We cannot retrieve content already seen or copied by a recipient.

3. Nearby discovery, device information, and connections

Sharebeam uses Apple's local-network and Bonjour services to discover and advertise nearby devices. Connection information can include your chosen device name, device category, a temporary random discovery identifier, service names, local network addresses and ports, and technical session capabilities. A default device name may come from your device or computer settings.

Your advertised name and discovery details may be visible to other devices on the local network or Apple's supported nearby peer-to-peer interfaces before a sharing session is approved. You can change the name in Sharebeam Settings. Do not use a personal name or other private information as your device name if you do not want it advertised.

During a connection, the devices exchange public keys, pairing information, random session identifiers, connection state, and technical media details such as frame dimensions and orientation. These details enable discovery, matching-code approval, encryption, compatibility, and delivery to approved viewers. Some session and peer details appear in the app's interface.

The direct-sharing feature does not send this information to a zxapps server. Network operators, device operating systems, and devices on the same network may be able to observe network metadata. Discovery advertisements do not have the same confidentiality as the encrypted media connection.

4. Information stored on your devices

Sharebeam stores preferences such as your device name, picture-quality choice, and onboarding completion. It also stores a local record that the one-time sharing trial has started, including a random session identifier and timing information. This prevents repeated use of the free trial.

On supported systems, local app storage and the shared container between Sharebeam and its broadcast extension hold operational records. These may contain peer names, endpoints, temporary connection credentials, viewer identifiers and status, error messages, session timing, and Pro-access or subscription-expiry information. They let the app and extension coordinate a broadcast, stop sharing, and enforce access limits. They are not a saved video or audio recording.

Local data is handled through Apple's app storage, sandbox, and operating-system protection mechanisms. Device backups or migrations may include app data according to your Apple settings. Sharebeam does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store downloads, in-app purchases, subscription payments, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. Sharebeam uses Apple's StoreKit framework to load products and verify access. The app can process transaction and product identifiers, product type, signed transaction dates, expiry or revocation information, and current entitlement status to unlock or restore Pro features.

Payment-card details, bank details, and your Apple Account password are not provided to zxapps by Sharebeam. We do not operate a separate purchase-verification server. Apple may make sales, subscription, proceeds, and other App Store reports available to us under its developer services; these are separate from your live sharing content.

Apple controls the information it retains about purchases. Removing Sharebeam does not cancel a subscription, remove Apple's purchase history, or necessarily prevent purchases from being restored. Manage subscriptions and refund requests through Apple. See App Store & Privacy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Diagnostics and support

Sharebeam does not contain a third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK and does not automatically upload its local session records to zxapps. Apple may collect device, usage, performance, or crash information through its operating systems and App Store services and may make reports available to developers according to your settings and Apple's terms. Such reports can contain technical details relevant to a problem.

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and attachments or technical details you choose to include. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, handle privacy requests, prevent abuse, and maintain necessary correspondence. Support email is handled through Google/Gmail and your own email provider. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, full screen recordings, or sensitive documents unless needed for the issue. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots and logs. We do not use support messages to send unrelated marketing without any consent required by law.

7. Who receives information

Information is made available to the recipients you choose for screen or audio sharing; Apple for operating-system features, App Store purchases, and developer services; Google and email providers for support correspondence; and GitHub for hosting this website. AirPlay sessions initiated through Apple's system controls are handled by Apple and your receiver, rather than Sharebeam's direct-sharing protocol. Receiver manufacturers and their services may have their own privacy practices.

We may use limited support or business records where necessary to meet a legal obligation, respond to a legally valid request, protect rights or safety, or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim. Any disclosure must have a lawful basis and be limited to what is necessary. If responsibility for Sharebeam changes, personal information held by us may be transferred only subject to applicable privacy requirements and appropriate notice.

Where a provider processes personal information on our behalf, we require appropriate limits on its use and safeguards consistent with applicable law and this policy. Independent services and people you choose to share with have their own responsibilities and privacy practices. We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

8. Retention and deletion

Live screen and audio data is held in temporary processing and playback buffers for the session. Sharebeam does not maintain a recording archive. Stopping sharing or ending the connection stops new media delivery; content already received cannot be recalled.

Preferences and the trial-used record persist locally until changed where supported or until the relevant app data is removed. One-use setup credentials are removed when consumed by the broadcast extension; other session records are cleared, replaced, or invalidated as sessions progress. Some status and trial records can remain after sharing ends. We do not promise that every local record is erased immediately when you stop sharing.

Support correspondence and related records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, handle follow-up, maintain security, and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of request, whether a dispute remains open, and any legal duty to retain a record. When those purposes end, we delete or anonymize information we control. Provider backups may take additional time to expire under the provider's retention practices.

You can ask us to delete support information by emailing the privacy contact. We cannot remotely access or erase the app data on your devices, another user's independent copies, Apple's records, or hosting-provider records that we do not control. Removing an app may not remove every shared-container, backup, or computer-support file. Contact us for help locating app data on your platform.

You control whether to start sharing, which devices to approve, whether supported device audio is shared, and when to stop. Compare the verification codes before approving a connection. Stop sharing or disconnect in the app, or use Apple's broadcast controls when a system broadcast is active. Denying or revoking Local Network or screen-capture access through the applicable system settings prevents the features that need it. You can still choose not to use those features. Permission names and controls vary by platform.

Depending on the law that applies and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a portable copy of personal information; object to certain processing; or withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Some laws also allow an authorized agent to act for you or let you appeal a denied request. Contact zxapps1993@gmail.com to make a request or appeal. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or authorization and will respond within applicable legal deadlines. We do not discriminate against people for exercising applicable privacy rights.

We do not build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or use personal information for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Children and other users should share only with trusted people and under appropriate supervision. A screen may contain information about other people; obtain any permissions required before displaying or sharing it. 13. Security

Direct Sharebeam media connections use encryption through Apple's CryptoKit framework and matching-code approval. Local operational records use app sandbox and operating-system protections where available. We limit information processed to what the described features need. Discovery metadata remains visible as described above, and AirPlay is governed by its own system implementation.

No device, network, or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your devices and apps updated, use trusted networks and recipients, and protect device access. Encryption does not prevent an approved recipient from seeing or copying what you share.

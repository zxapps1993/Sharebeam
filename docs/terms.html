2. What the app does

Sharebeam sends a screen from a supported iPhone, iPad, or Mac to nearby approved devices running a compatible Sharebeam version. Apple TV is a receiving device. Some features require a Pro purchase. Devices normally need the same suitable local network, although Apple's supported peer-to-peer networking may also be used. The app does not provide an internet relay, remote device control, or a built-in recording archive.

AirPlay discovery lists advertised services and provides guidance. Actual system AirPlay mirroring is selected and managed through Apple's controls. Support for a particular TV, receiver, network, protected video service, application, or audio route is not guaranteed. A TV without a compatible receiving method may need separate hardware or software. Sharebeam is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple or a TV manufacturer merely because it works with their devices.

3. Your sharing choices

Approve only devices and people you trust, and confirm that the verification codes match. A device name is not proof of someone's identity. Your advertised device name and some discovery details may be visible to nearby network participants before pairing.

Before starting, check what will be shared. A whole-screen broadcast may include notifications and content from other apps as you switch between them. If supported device audio is enabled, recipients can hear it through their device's current output. Sharebeam does not use or transmit microphone audio; its broadcast extension discards any microphone samples supplied by the system.

The app needs the local-network and screen-capture permissions used by the relevant feature. Apple's system controls determine what capture is allowed. Denying or withdrawing a permission can make the related feature unavailable. You can stop sharing or disconnect in the app; for a system broadcast, Apple's broadcast controls also stop it. Merely switching apps is not a reliable way to stop a whole-screen broadcast.

Once information has reached a recipient, stopping sharing cannot recall it. Recipients may take photographs, record, or otherwise copy content independently of Sharebeam. Encryption protects the direct media connection but does not prevent an approved recipient from seeing, hearing, or copying its contents. Networks and devices are not completely secure.

5. Free use and Pro features

Receiving a screen is free. The current app offers one free screen-sharing session of up to two minutes. Starting actual capture marks the trial as used locally. Stopping early or relaunching does not reset that record. The free share is not a subscription, does not start automatic billing, and does not require agreeing to a paid plan.

Sharebeam Pro provides unlimited sharing duration, shared device audio, higher picture quality, and up to four approved viewers, subject to supported hardware, compatible app versions, and network and operating-system limits. Sending audio is directed to one chosen receiving device at a time. These limits and supported features are explained in the app and may evolve with updates.

Available plans may include weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, or annual subscriptions and a lifetime purchase. Availability is determined by the products Apple returns for your storefront. The purchase sheet and product information shown by Apple specify the actual price, currency, billing period, and applicable conditions. No purchase is made simply by opening the app or viewing plans.
